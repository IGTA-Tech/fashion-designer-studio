# 👗 Welcome to Your Fashion Studio!

## What is this?

This is YOUR personal AI design assistant! It helps you:
- Turn your sketches into realistic photos
- Get expert sewing advice
- See your designs in different colors and styles
- Learn how to construct the clothes you design

## How to Use It (Super Simple!)

### Step 1: Upload Your Sketch

1. Draw your design idea on paper
2. Take a photo with your phone
3. Upload it by:
   - Dragging the photo into the upload box
   - OR clicking "Choose Files"

💡 **Tip**: You can upload up to 4 photos at once (front, back, side, details)

### Step 2: Chat with Your AI Assistant

After uploading, your AI assistant (powered by Claude) will analyze your design and ask questions like:
- "What fabric are you thinking?"
- "What colors did you have in mind?"
- "Is this for day or evening wear?"

Just type your answers in the chat! It's like texting with a fashion mentor.

### Step 3: See Your Design Come to Life

Click the buttons on the right side:

- **🎨 Different Colors** - See your design in 4 color options
- **📐 Different Angles** - See front, back, side views
- **👗 On Dress Form** - See it on YOUR actual dress form!
- **✨ Styled Look** - See it on a model

The AI will generate photorealistic images based on your sketch!

### Step 4: Get Help Making It

Ask in the chat: **"How do I make this?"**

You'll get:
- Complete materials list
- Where to buy everything
- Step-by-step instructions
- Budget estimate
- Pro tips

You can print or save this guide!

### Step 5: Save Your Project

Click "💾 Save Project" at the top to save everything:
- Your sketches
- All generated images
- Your conversation
- Your notes

Come back anytime and pick up where you left off!

## Tips for Best Results

✅ **Good Photo Quality**
- Draw clearly on white/light paper
- Good lighting (natural light is best)
- Photo taken straight-on, not at an angle
- Include as much detail as you can

✅ **Be Specific**
- Mention colors you're thinking about
- Say what fabric you want to use
- Tell the AI your skill level if you want advice
- Describe the occasion (casual, formal, etc.)

✅ **Experiment!**
- Try different color combinations
- Generate multiple versions
- Ask "what if" questions
- Save the ones you like

## Troubleshooting

**"My image won't upload"**
- Make sure it's a JPG, PNG, or WEBP file
- File must be under 10MB
- Try taking a new photo

**"The AI doesn't understand my sketch"**
- Make sure lines are clear and dark
- Try describing it in chat: "This is a maxi dress with..."
- Upload multiple angles if possible

**"Generated images don't look like my sketch"**
- Be more specific in your description
- Try the custom prompt option
- Add details about style, fit, and features

**"I can't find my saved project"**
- Check the left sidebar
- Use the search box to find it by name
- Projects are saved in your browser (don't clear browser data!)
